Research

I work at the interface of mitochondrial biology, immune engineering, and cell and molecular biology, looking for the mechanisms behind cardiovascular disease, obesity and metabolic liver disease, macrophage metabolism, inflammation and sepsis.

Proposed research: mitochondria-based precision therapeutics for cardiovascular and metabolic disease

Cardiovascular disease is the leading cause of death in the United States, and obesity and metabolic dysfunction are major risk factors for it. Mitochondria sit at the center of cellular metabolism, and calcium and magnesium, the two most common divalent cations, act as second messengers that control mitochondrial bioenergetics. When their dynamics go wrong, mitochondria fail and vascular inflammation follows. My plan is to work out how mechanotransduction, intracellular and mitochondrial calcium, and metabolic reprogramming drive endothelial and vascular differentiation, and to build a platform that combines metabolic reprogramming with mitochondrial transfer and replacement therapy.

  1. Mechanotransduction in endothelial differentiation. In myeloid precursor cells from mouse bone marrow and human blood, laminar shear stress activates mechanosensitive channels and drives calcium influx, lipid deposition, lower oxidative phosphorylation and higher glycolysis. Flow cytometry showed more CD34, VEGFR1, VEGFR2, Piezo1 and Orai1, and less CD163, PINK1 and PARKIN.
  2. Mitochondrial transfer between cells. In cells expressing the mitochondrial calcium sensor MitoGCaMP6f, shear stress produced tunneling nanotubes that moved mitochondria from healthy donor cells to stressed recipients. The hypothesis is that mechanotransduction can trigger this transfer to rescue cells from metabolic stress.
  3. Exogenous mitochondrial transplantation. Healthy mitochondria isolated from expanded myeloid precursor cells of young mice would be transplanted into mice predisposed to cardiovascular disease, to improve lipid use and metabolic energetics and slow disease.
  4. Implications for aging. Mechanical stimulation for 24 hours raised CD34 in mature myeloid cells, which suggests they can revert toward a precursor state. Next I will study CD300c, CD150, DNMTs, HMGBs and sirtuins, together with mitochondrial transfer, as ways to improve mitochondrial health and slow aging in models of cardiovascular disease and cancer.

Research highlights

Mitochondrial magnesium

Lactate triggers magnesium release from the ER and its uptake into mitochondria through Mrs2, and blocking the mitochondrial surge reduces inflammation-induced multi-organ failure. In Mrs2 knockout mice, limiting uptake reprogrammed metabolism, improved hepatocyte bioenergetics and protected against fatty liver on a western diet, through HIF1α and citrate.Cell, 2020; Cell Reports, 2023

ER magnesium transport

ERMA (TMEM94) is a P-type ATPase that moves magnesium into the ER. Reduced ERMA expression leads to cardiac dysfunction and abnormal calcium cycling in cardiomyocytes, including iPSC-derived cells.Molecular Cell, 2024; Cell Reports, 2026

Off-the-shelf vascular grafts

In Prof. Andreadis’s lab, acellular grafts implanted in sheep carotid arteries for 6 and 12 weeks became functional neo-arteries without occlusion. CX3CR1-Confetti lineage tracing, single-cell RNA-seq and Visium spatial transcriptomics showed monocytes becoming macrophages, then endothelial-like cells at the lumen. A newer study compares young and aged hosts.npj Regenerative Medicine, 2025 and 2026

Macrophage mitochondria and calcium

Shear stress on macrophages raised calcium influx and lipid deposition and lowered oxidative phosphorylation, pushing them toward a glycolytic, pro-inflammatory state. Pharmacological MCU activators restored mitochondrial function, cleared lipid and resolved inflammation. Calcium overload that shuts off mitochondrial uptake also drives foam cell formation in atherosclerosis models.

Mitochondrial calcium and bioenergetics

Pyruvate and fatty acid flux tune MICU1 control of the MCU. miR-195 represses MICU1 in ovarian cancer. The MCU inhibitors RU265 and Compound C2 protected cells from hypoxia and reoxygenation injury.Science Signaling, 2020; EMBO Reports, 2020; ACS Central Science, 2019

SARS-CoV-2 and the heart

RNA-seq of human blood cells found that viral proteins target the mitochondrial permeability transition pore genes CCDC58 and SPG7, inhibiting mitochondrial function and disrupting cardiomyocyte calcium oscillations.iScience, 2022

Necroptosis and SREBP

My current project uses lipidomics, RNA-seq, mass spectrometry and imaging to follow SREBP as it moves from the ER to the Golgi and nucleus during necroptosis, and to find ways to limit pore formation in this form of cell death.ACS Chemical Biology, 2026

Cancer pharmacotherapeutics

Work on the TRPM7 agonist Naltriben, covalent taccalonolide microtubule stabilizers, the TRPC1/4/5 agonist Englerin A in breast cancer cells, and bioactive peptides from ascidians and a cuttlefish toxin in animal models.NPJ Precision Oncology, 2025; Nature Communications, 2020; Breast Cancer Research and Treatment, 2019; Chemico-Biological Interactions, 2017; Int.
